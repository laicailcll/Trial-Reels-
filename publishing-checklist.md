# Trial Reel preparation and review

## Before publishing

- [ ] Write the audience question this video should explore.
- [ ] Check whether the opening makes sense without your biography or earlier posts.
- [ ] Identify the creative choice you are testing.
- [ ] Confirm that Trial is enabled in the publishing flow.
- [ ] Record whether automatic sharing is enabled.
- [ ] Save the publication time and timezone.

## At review

- [ ] Record the review time and the metric labels actually visible in your app.
- [ ] Leave unavailable information blank; distinguish it from a displayed zero.
- [ ] Note responses that address the original audience question.
- [ ] Record other differences between trials, including topic and timing.
- [ ] State what the evidence cannot establish.
- [ ] Choose an action: keep observing, share more widely, or revise a future video.
- [ ] Write the reason for the action without inventing a pass mark.

## Boundaries

A trial is not a confidentiality setting. Responses from unfamiliar viewers cannot by themselves establish the reaction of existing followers. This checklist is an editorial planning aid, not a platform rule or a promise of reach.
